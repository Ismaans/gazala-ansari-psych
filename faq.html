Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about therapy, scheduling, and what to expect.

Do I need a referral or diagnosis to book?

No referral is needed. You can contact me directly.

Is therapy confidential?

Yes. Therapists are required by law to keep confidential anything their clients share. Confidentiality can only be broken if there is a belief that a client may harm themselves or others.

Do you offer telehealth/online sessions?

Yes. I offer both in-person and telehealth sessions. Please contact my office to confirm your eligibility.

What ages do you work with?

I work with clients ages 4 and up, including children (ages 6–10), teens, and adults.

What languages are sessions available in?

Sessions can be conducted in English, Hindi, Spanish, or Urdu.

Do you accept insurance?

Yes. BlueCross and BlueShield is accepted. Out-of-network documentation can be provided for other insurers.

What is the difference between a psychologist and a psychiatrist?

A psychologist holds a doctoral degree (PhD or PsyD) and specializes in therapy, assessment, and evaluation. A psychiatrist is a medical doctor who primarily prescribes medication. I am a psychologist and do not prescribe medication.

What should I expect at my first appointment?

Your first session is a chance for me to understand your concerns, personal history, and goals. Together, we will discuss the best approach for your needs and determine whether my practice is the right fit.

Crisis Resources

This website is not for emergencies. If you or someone you know is in immediate danger, call 911. For mental health crises, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline). You may also text HOME to 741741 to reach the Crisis Text Line.
