Who I Am

I am a clinical psychologist, neuropsychologist, forensic psychologist, and intellectual & developmental disabilities psychologist with over 14 years of experience. I earned my Doctor of Philosophy in Psychology from American University in 2006 and have been licensed in the State of Virginia since 2010.

My Approach

I work diligently with my clients to identify and examine maladaptive patterns that cause obstacles to living well. Through this process, the elements needed for positive change — such as insight and acceptance — materialize and help move one toward a more healthy and fulfilling life. I place great value on the relationship between therapist and client, and strive to create a safe, comfortable, confidential, and nonjudgmental space.

My therapy draws on multiple evidence-based frameworks:

  • Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT)
  • Psychodynamic Therapy
  • Insight-Oriented Therapy
  • Eclectic/Integrative Approaches
  • Psychological Testing & Evaluation

Background

Prior to private practice, I worked at universities, the Women's Center in Virginia, and at schools throughout the DMV area, providing therapy and conducting evaluations for ADHD/ADD, learning disabilities, emotional challenges, and neuropsychological issues for clients ages 4 and up.
