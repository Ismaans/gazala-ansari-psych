About Dr. Apyan

She works with children, adolescents, adults, and families navigating life's most challenging chapters: grief and loss, major life transitions, aging and caregiver stress, and family conflict, all in a non-judgmental environment. Her approach is client-centered and strengths-based, meeting clients where they are and collaborating with them toward the life they envision.

She is a Licensed Master Social Worker with over a decade of clinical and leadership experience, specializing in caregiver stress among the "sandwiched" generation, senior mental health, low motivation, family conflict, anxiety, and ADHD. She believes every person, at every stage of life, deserves a safe space to be heard, supported, and reminded of their own strength.

She holds a Master of Social Work with a Concentration in Geriatrics from the University of Southern California (2015) and is fluent in English and French, with a deep commitment to culturally competent, patient-centered care.

Prior to joining Ansari & Associates, Emily served as Vice President of Operations at Elder Tree Care Management in Northern Virginia, overseeing care for hundreds of older adults and their families through one of the region's largest care management organizations. She previously served as Executive Director at Sunrise of Alexandria, leading a team of 65 staff and 8 department coordinators supporting 110 residents in Assisted Living and Memory Care, and has held roles as an Assisted Living and Social Services Manager and direct Social Worker. This experience gives her an unusually deep, firsthand understanding of the systems, stressors, and decisions that families face when caring for aging loved ones.
