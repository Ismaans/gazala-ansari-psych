About Dr. Stancell

Dr. Waltrina is an independently licensed clinician in Washington DC, Maryland, and Virginia with over ten years of experience in mental health and addictions counseling. She specializes in working with children, adolescents, adults, and families, using a person-centered and cognitive behavioral approach to explore thought patterns behind challenging emotions and behaviors.

She provides a warm, safe environment for clients to process their thoughts and feelings, working collaboratively to identify and mediate stressors, and is a strong advocate for self-care. She looks forward to learning about each client's experiences and personal goals.

She holds a Master of Arts in Clinical Psychology from The Chicago School of Professional Psychology in Washington DC (2019), a Master of Arts in Mental Health and Behavioral Medicine from Boston University School of Medicine (2009), and a Bachelor of Science in Psychology from Howard University (2007). She is a Board Approved Clinical Supervisor recognized by the State of Maryland DHMH Board of Professional Counselors and Therapists, authorized to supervise LGPCs.

Her clinical career spans over a decade of practice, supervision, and leadership across the DMV area, including work as a Contract Therapist at Inceptus Psychological & Consulting Services in Alexandria, a Practicum and Internship Supervisor at The Chicago School of Professional Psychology, and a Program Manager overseeing adolescent outpatient and intensive outpatient substance abuse treatment at Federal City Recovery Services in Washington DC. She has been a valued member of the Ansari & Associates team since 2021.
