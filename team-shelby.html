About Dr. Grant-Workman

Life is a journey of ups and downs. If you're facing uncertainty, feeling anxious or down, or navigating difficult transitions, you are not alone. Seeking support is a brave step toward finding change and fulfillment in your life.

With a background as a preschool teacher and as a licensed clinical social worker, I bring a unique set of skills to create a safe space for children, teens, caregivers, and adults. In my practice, I utilize an eclectic approach and believe in the power of a strong therapeutic alliance with clients.

I am passionate about working with children and their caregivers, blending my preschool and therapy experiences. Using skill-building and play therapy techniques, I foster a nurturing space for children to express and navigate difficult emotions while supporting parents through life challenges.

With the many stressors in the world today, I support clients with anxiety and depression by developing strategies for coping and achieving a better balance. I also assist families navigating aging-related transitions.
