Welcome

Ansari & Associates has been part of the Alexandria community since 2006. Over nearly two decades, we've built a strong reputation — most of our clients come to us through word of mouth and referrals from people we've already helped. We value quality, individualized, patient-centered care, meeting each client where they are and supporting lasting change.

Based in Alexandria, we serve clients throughout the greater DMV area, including Arlington, Fairfax, and Falls Church, with both in-person and telehealth appointments.

Our team brings together clinical psychologists, licensed social workers, and professional counselors under one roof — a multidisciplinary approach that lets us support children, adolescents, adults, and families across a wide range of needs, from therapy to comprehensive psychological and neuropsychological evaluation.

Your initial 15-minute phone consultation is always free. Give us a call at (703) 224-8983 — we're here to help.

Services Offered

  • Individual Psychotherapy
  • Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT)
  • Psychodynamic Therapy
  • Insight-Oriented Therapy
  • Psychological & Neuropsychological Testing and Evaluation
  • ADHD & Learning Disability Evaluations (ages 4+)
  • Forensic Psychological Assessment
  • Counseling for somatic complaints (IBS, fibromyalgia, seizures, headaches/migraines)
  • Psychoeducational Evaluation and Consultation
