Privacy Policy

How we handle information collected through this website.

Overview

Ansari & Associates, LLC ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy. This policy describes how information may be collected and used when you visit this website or submit the contact form.

Information We Collect

When you use our contact form, we may collect your name, email address, phone number, reason for inquiry, preferred contact method, and message content. Please do not submit sensitive health information through the website contact form.

How We Use Information

Information submitted through the contact form is used solely to respond to your inquiry and coordinate scheduling. We do not sell your personal information.

Confidentiality of Clinical Records

Information shared in therapy or evaluation is governed by separate clinical confidentiality policies and applicable law. Communications through this website contact form are not a substitute for establishing a therapeutic relationship.

Cookies & Analytics

This website does not currently use tracking cookies or third-party analytics. If this changes, this policy will be updated.

Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information submitted through this site. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.

Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, contact us at (703) 224-8983 or through our contact page.

Crisis Resources

This website is not for emergencies. If you or someone you know is in immediate danger, call 911. For mental health crises, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline). You may also text HOME to 741741 to reach the Crisis Text Line.
